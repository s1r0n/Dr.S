<img srce="https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/71ymZTPaSmS._SY522_.jpg" width=250 align=right>I am downloading this PDF for my studies at Athabasca University, but I do want to thank you for writing the [Rocket Scientists' guide to money and the economy](https://www.amazon.com/Rocket-Scientists-Guide-Money-Economy/dp/1897455119/ref=tmm_pap_swatch_0). I have thoroughly enjoyed that book. It's like you are vaguely aware that things don't match up with society and you are confused why there are rich people ignoring the starving children and homeless, but you wrote about it in a way that was easy to follow and broke down the steps of their process. Thank you for that. I am sharing that book with everyone I know.

Thank you for your honesty and vulnerability when speaking about your own history. You create space for others to feel safe doing the same.
