Here I trace how money, often framed as a neutral medium of exchange, functions as a mechanism of exploitation and control. I argue that money erodes authentic human relationships, distorts social values, and drives environmental destruction. I call for a fundamental re-evaluation of monetary systems and invite readers to imagine alternatives that prioritize human and planetary well-being over profit.
