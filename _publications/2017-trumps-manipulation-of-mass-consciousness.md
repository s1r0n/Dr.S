In this article, I analyze Donald Trump’s political communication through the lens of mass psychology. I argue that he manipulates collective fears, exploits cultural archetypes, and mobilizes resentment to consolidate his authority. I connect Trump’s success to broader structural dynamics of inequality and disempowerment, warning that these dynamics must be addressed if we are to resist authoritarian manipulation.
